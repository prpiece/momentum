# Spec: v1.5 Pro plan

Decisions: D266, D259. Depends on v1.4 (the `plan_limits` function and its call sites).

## Goal

People who need more devices, bigger shares or more storage can pay for Pro. Everything local stays free.

## Plans

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Every local feature (matrix, calendar, habits, focus, voice, CLI/MCP, import, One thing) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Synced devices | 2 | Unlimited |
| Members per share | 3 | 25 |
| Attachment storage | 100 MB | 10 GB |
| Connections (bots, webhook, calendar subscriptions) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Two-way Google and Outlook sync (once built) | | ✓ |

Price: **$5.99 a month**, or **$61.10 a year** (15% off twelve months).

## How it works

- **Lemon Squeezy** is the merchant of record and handles VAT and sales tax (settles D190). Checkout opens in the system browser. A webhook to an Edge Function, with its signature verified, writes `subscriptions(account, status, plan, renews_at)`.
- **One source of truth:** `plan_limits(account)` reads `subscriptions`. Device registration, share invites and uploads already call it (v1.4), so no limit is checked anywhere else.
- **Going over a limit after a downgrade:** nothing is deleted.
  - Existing members and files stay.
  - New invites, uploads or devices are refused with a message naming the limit.
  - The oldest extra device is signed out after a 7-day notice.
- **Settings → Account** shows the plan, what it includes, Upgrade, and Manage billing (Lemon Squeezy's customer portal).
- **Development builds** keep returning the Pro limits for every account. Release builds read the real plan.

## Before launch (D259)

These land with or before v1.5. Payment is the first real spending:
- the domain, the website and the hosted privacy page;
- sending email through a provider with a verified domain;
- the store accounts;
- Google's app verification for two-way sync.

## Verification

- Webhook tests: a bad signature is refused; created, renewed, cancelled and expired events all map to the right status; replays are idempotent.
- Limit tests at each boundary for Free and Pro, and for a downgrade with too many devices, members or files.
- Real-app check: buy Pro in Lemon Squeezy's test mode, invite a fourth member, cancel, and confirm the fifth invite is refused while the existing members stay.

## Acceptance

- No local feature ever checks the plan.
- Every limit is enforced on the server and explained in the UI.
