# Spec: v1.2 Friends and leaderboards

Phase D, which now ships before sharing (D181). Decisions: D145–D152, D180, D182, D184. Depends on v1.1 (accounts, sync, profiles).

## Goal

"The planner you do with friends." Signed-in users add friends and compare a weekly score that is fair and hard to fake. This is the reason to choose Momentum over TickTick and Todoist, so the friend loop has to work without friction.

## Out of scope

- A global leaderboard.
- Chat, comments and reactions (later, if wanted).
- Push notifications on mobile.
- Payments.

## Friends

- **Friendship is mutual,** stored as `friendships(a, b, status: pending|accepted, requested_by, created_at)` with `a < b`, so each pair appears only once.
- **Adding a friend:**
  - Enter a @handle to send a request, which the other person accepts or declines.
  - Or open someone's **invite link**.
- **Invite link** (host per D189 until the domain exists):
  - The URL is on GitHub Pages until the domain exists (D189), then `https://usemomentum.<tld>/i/<code>` (D192). The page opens the app through `momentum://invite/<code>` and, when the app isn't installed, shows the download page.
  - Each code belongs to one sender and can be revoked. It expires after 30 days.
  - Opening it while signed in sends a request that is accepted at once.
  - Opening it while signed out saves the code and applies it right after sign-up.
- **Limits:**
  - 20 requests a day per account, enforced on the server.
  - At most 200 friends.
  - Once declined, the same pair can't send another request for 7 days.
- **Block:**
  - Ends the friendship and cancels pending requests in both directions.
  - Stops invites and requests from the blocked account.
  - Hides each account from the other's search and leaderboard.
  - Leaves shared memberships unchanged (v1.3).
  - Stored in `blocks(blocker, blocked)`.
- **Report:** `reports(reporter, target, reason, created_at)` covers handle or display-name abuse. Review is manual for now, through the Supabase dashboard.
- **When a friend deletes their account,** they disappear from friend lists and leaderboards.

## Weekly score (D147–D148)

- **The server computes it** in the SQL function `weekly_score(account, week_start)` and caches the result in `scores(owner_id, week_start, points, breakdown jsonb, computed_at)`. The function runs again whenever relevant rows change.
- **The week runs Monday to Sunday** in the **account's** time zone. The zone is stored in `profiles.tz` and updated from the device when it changes.
  - Friends in different zones each have their own week. The leaderboard compares everyone's current week.
  - Changing time zone in the middle of a week affects only the days that follow.
- **Points:**
  - **+1 per task completed,** when `completed_by = me`.
    - The completion counts on the server **receipt** day, not the client's `completed_at`, so backdating doesn't help.
    - The task must have existed for at least 10 minutes on the server before it was completed.
    - At most 20 a day.
    - Un-completing a task removes its point.
  - **+1 per 5 focus minutes:**
    - Sessions can't overlap, and an overlapping session counts only once.
    - A session can't last longer than 24 hours.
    - At most 8 hours a day.
  - **+2 per habit check-in that meets its goal.** One per habit per day.
  - **+10 per perfect day,** using the existing perfect-day rule. It's evaluated on the server from synced rows plus `rest_days`.
- **Lifetime trophies stay client-side** and unchanged. Only the weekly score is computed on the server.
- **The perfect-day and streak rules exist twice,** in `src/domain/perfect.ts` and in SQL. A shared fixture of test cases runs against both so they can't drift apart.

## What friends can see (D149)

- **The `public_profile` view shows** handle, display name, avatar, this week's score and its breakdown by category, current streak, perfect days this week, and the top level of each trophy kind.
- **Clients sync trophy levels** in `profiles.trophies jsonb`, as numbers only.
- **Never shown:** any task, list or habit name, or any timestamp more detailed than a day.
- **Privacy switches** in Settings → Account:
  - "Hide me from leaderboards": you still see the board, but others don't see you on it.
  - "Hide my profile stats": friends see only your handle and avatar.
- **RLS:** a row of `public_profile` and `scores` is readable only by the account itself and its accepted friends who aren't blocked.

## Progress page (designed in v1.0, filled in now)

- **The Friends section has:**
  - this week's leaderboard (rank, avatar, handle, score, and a small up or down arrow compared with yesterday);
  - your own row highlighted;
  - the days left in the week.
- **Selecting a friend** opens their public profile card.
- **Buttons:** "Add friend" (by handle or by copying the invite link) and a requests inbox with a badge.
- **The Share card** shows your @handle and invite link when you're signed in.
- **Getting started** gains "Add a friend" (D184) for signed-in users, and "New:" entries for upgraders (D178).
- **The tour** gains one Friends slide, still 6 slides at most.

## Notifications (D151)

- **Badge:** the Progress navigation item shows a badge for pending requests.
- **Native notifications** for a new friend request and an accepted request.
- **"You were overtaken by @x"** is off by default. It's a switch under Notifications, and fires at most once a day.
- **Monday recap:** an in-app card at the top of Progress ("Last week: 2nd of 5"). It's always on, with no native notification.
- **Delivery:** Realtime on the `friendships` and `scores` rows of your friends drives the badges.

## Edge cases

- **Two people send requests to each other at once:** the second request accepts the first, through a unique pair and an upsert.
- **A request is sent to a handle that then changes:** requests are keyed by account id, so it still works.
- **A handle is freed after a rename:** it can't be reused for 30 days, so friends aren't confused.
- **A completion arrives late after a long offline stretch:** it counts on the day the server receives it, which may be the following week. This is accepted and documented in Help.
- **A clock skewed by days:** it doesn't matter, because receipt time is the server's.
- **Points from a task that's later deleted:** they're removed when the task is deleted, and the score is recomputed.
- **The week boundary:** the Monday recap freezes the previous week's `scores` row once 24 hours have passed in the account's zone.

## Verification

- **SQL tests:** score caps, overlapping focus sessions, the receipt-day rule, un-complete and delete, and the time-zone boundary. The perfect-day fixture is shared with `perfect.test.ts`.
- **RLS suite:**
  - a stranger can't see scores or profiles;
  - a blocked friend can't see either;
  - a hidden account is missing from friends' boards;
  - request limits are enforced.
- **Real-app check:** two accounts become friends through an invite link, complete tasks, and see each other's score update within 10 seconds.

## Acceptance

- A friendship goes from invite link to "on my leaderboard" in under a minute.
- Scores match the SQL definition, and no client-supplied number is trusted.
- Friends never see any content names.
