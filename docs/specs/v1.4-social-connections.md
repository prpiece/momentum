# Spec: v1.4 Friends, shares and connections (Track C)

Decisions: D258–D263, D265, D266 (caps). ADRs 0004, 0005, 0008. Depends on v1.1 sync; it merges after v1.3, because the assignee joins the `Task` type that Track B owns.

## Goal

Momentum becomes something you can use with other people and from the apps you already live in:
- Friends send each other tasks, share lists and folders, focus together and keep each other honest on habits.
- Anyone can be invited by link.
- Discord, Slack, Telegram and a webhook drop tasks straight into your Inbox, and the bots can show and update your day.

There are no scores or rankings anywhere (D256).

## Out of scope

- Payments and plan enforcement beyond the cap constants (v1.5). Until then every account gets the Pro limits (D266).
- Chat, comments, reactions and live presence beyond Focus together.
- Reading ordinary chat messages ("@Momentum …"): slash commands and direct messages only (ADR 0008).
- Sharing habits, events or countdowns.

## Track C owns

- Every Supabase migration in this phase, including the server columns Track B handed off (deadline, duration, Someday, waiting, habit period). That migration goes first.
- Edge Functions, RLS and the RLS test suite.
- Settings → Connections, which mounts Track B's AI agents and calendar subscription panels.

## Friends

- **Friendship is mutual,** stored as `friendships(a, b, status: pending|accepted, requested_by, created_at)` with `a < b`.
- **Adding a friend:** by @handle request, or through an invite link.
- **Limits:** 20 requests a day, 200 friends, and a 7-day wait after a decline. They're enforced on the server.
- **Block** ends the friendship, cancels requests both ways, and hides each account from the other's search. Shares aren't touched.
- **Report** is stored in `reports` and reviewed by hand in the Supabase dashboard.
- **The Friends page** (Browse on the phone; a section beside Progress on desktop) lists friends, requests with a badge, Add friend, and Copy invite link.

### What friends do (D260)

- **Sent task:**
  - The sender picks Send to friend on a task. The recipient sees it in a Requests area and, on accepting, gets a copy in their Inbox (title, note, checklist and subtasks; attachments once shares exist).
  - The two copies aren't connected afterwards. The sender sees only Accepted or Declined.
  - It goes through the inbound function (ADR 0008).
- **Invite to a share** in one tap from the friend list.
- **Assign** tasks in a share to friends who are members.
- **Focus together:**
  - "Let friends see when I'm focusing" (off by default) publishes only the fact that a session is running and when it ends, through Realtime.
  - **Join** starts your own session ending at the same moment.
  - Nobody sees what task the other is working on.
- **Check-in buddies:**
  - On a habit, choose friends who see its name and today's check-in, nothing else.
  - A buddy can **nudge** at most once a day, which arrives as a native notification. Nudges can be switched off per habit.

## Invite links (D261)

- **One link type:**
  - `momentum://invite/<code>`, through the GitHub Pages redirect page until the domain exists;
  - it makes the opener a friend, or a member of a share with the role recorded on the link.
- **Expiry and revoking:** friend links last 30 days and share links 7. Only their creator can revoke them.
- **Opening a link:**
  - while signed out, the app keeps the code and applies it after sign-up;
  - while the app isn't installed, the page shows the download links.
- **No invite email.**

## Shares (D262, ADR 0004)

Carried over from the earlier sharing design:
- **Members:** `share_members(container_type, container_id, account_id, role: owner|editor|viewer)`.
- **Access adds up** from the list and from up to 3 shared folders above it, through one `can_access(list_id, min_role)` that backs every RLS and storage policy.
- **Personal layer:** `task_personal(task_id, account_id, reminders, tags, pinned)` and `container_placement`. Personal fields never write the shared task, and tags on shared tasks are personal only.
- **Roles:**
  - owners manage membership, rename or delete the share, and move lists out;
  - editors change contents;
  - viewers only see the share and use their personal layer.
  - Triggers enforce this on the server.
- **Moving across the boundary:** a confirmation names who gains access. Moving a task out is limited to its creator or the owner. A personal folder can't go into a shared one.
- **Losing access:** rows are purged on the next pull, assignments are cleared, and personal rows are deleted. The owner must transfer ownership before leaving. When an owner deletes their account, ownership passes to the longest-standing editor.
- **Assignee:** `tasks.assignee_id`, which must be an editor or the owner. Today shows your tasks plus shared tasks assigned to you or to nobody, and a list filter offers "Assigned to me".
- **Activity:** an `actor_id` on activity, an Activity tab in the task drawer, and unread dots on shared lists.
- **Attachments:**
  - PNG, JPG, GIF and WebP only, checked by magic bytes, with metadata stripped;
  - a private bucket `share/<share_id>/…` with 1-hour signed URLs;
  - they count against the uploader's storage.
- **Caps:** members per share and storage come from one server-side `plan_limits(account)` function returning the Pro limits for everyone until v1.5.

## Connections (D263, D265, ADR 0008)

- **One function, `inbound`,** with an adapter per source:
  - Discord: HTTP interactions, Ed25519 verification, user-install and server-install, `/task …`;
  - Slack: slash command `/momentum …`, signing-secret verification, OAuth install without the Marketplace;
  - Telegram: a bot webhook, plain messages;
  - Webhook: a per-account secret URL taking `{ "text": "…" }`, add only;
  - Sent tasks.
- **Every source parses with `src/domain/quickAdd.ts`.** The function bundles the shared domain code, and a test fails if it imports anything that isn't pure.
- **Linking:** Settings → Connections shows a one-time 6-digit code (valid 10 minutes). `/task link <code>` binds that chat identity to the account, and `unlink` removes it. An unlinked chat only gets instructions.
- **Commands** (D265):

  | Command | Does |
  |---|---|
  | `add <text>` | Adds a task |
  | `today` · `week` · `overdue` · `inbox` | Lists those tasks, numbered |
  | `list <name>` · `search <text>` · `waiting` | Shows a list, searches, or shows Waiting for tasks |
  | `done N` · `snooze N <when>` · `move N <list>` · `deadline N <date>` | Changes a task |
  | `habit <name>` | Checks in a habit |
  | `send @friend <text>` | Sends a task to a friend |
  | `focus` | Shows the focus status |
  | `digest on <time>` · `digest off` | Turns the Digest on or off |
  | `help` · `unlink` | Help, or disconnect |

  `N` refers to the numbers in that chat's last listing, stored for 1 hour.
- **Privacy:** replies are always private (ephemeral, a DM, or the private chat) and show titles only.
- **Speed:** each source answers within 3 seconds; slower work is deferred and followed up.
- **Rate limits:** 60 commands an hour per linked identity, and 500 webhook calls a day per account.
- **Digest:** `pg_cron` sends each linked chat its Digest at the chosen local time (the account's time zone).
- **Keep-alive:** a GitHub Actions cron pings the project weekly so the free plan never pauses (D259).

## Verification

- **RLS and storage suite** against a fixture:
  - accounts: an owner, a folder editor, a folder viewer, a list-only member, a friend, a stranger and a blocked account;
  - checks every table and bucket operation against the role table, plus friend, buddy and focus-status visibility.
- **Sync simulation:** membership and role changes, moves across the boundary, sent tasks.
- **Function tests:**
  - a bad signature is refused for every source;
  - linking codes expire;
  - every command against a fixture account, including replies only the user can see;
  - rate limits.
- **Real-app check:**
  - two accounts become friends by link, send a task, share a folder, focus together and nudge;
  - `/task add` from Discord, `/momentum today` from Slack and a Telegram message each land in the right Inbox within 10 seconds.

## Acceptance

- No path lets an account see content outside its access, including attachments, focus status and habits.
- Every role-table row and every command is covered by a passing test.
- A new user can link Discord and add a task in under a minute.
