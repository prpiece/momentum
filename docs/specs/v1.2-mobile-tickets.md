# Tickets: v1.2 iOS and Android

Built from [v1.2-mobile.md](v1.2-mobile.md) (D228). Planning only: nothing here is designed or coded yet. Work them later, one ticket at a time, in the order of the graph below.

## How to work these tickets

- **Design tickets (`D*`)** run with **`/ai-mobile-product-design-director`**. They follow its gates, and each ends with an approval from you before the next starts:
  - the app already has a phone layout (D200), so use the skill's **redesign mode**: inventory what's observed, clutter audit, reduction plan, then wireframes;
  - no design ticket produces final visuals before the wireframes are approved;
  - every deliverable passes the skill's anti-slop gate (`references/mobile-anti-slop.md`).

  Design outputs go to `docs/design/mobile/`. Reference screenshots go there too, never into the app.
- **Code tickets (`C*`)** run with **`/ponytail:ponytail`**:
  - Before writing code, climb its ladder and write the answer in the ticket's PR: does it need to exist, is it already in the codebase, does the platform or an installed dependency cover it?
  - Each ticket's **Ladder notes** give the expected answer; challenge them if the code says otherwise.
  - Keep the smallest diff, and leave one check behind for every piece of real logic.
  - Follow CLAUDE.md: pure logic in `src/domain/` with a test beside it, decisions recorded in PLAN.md, one commit per ticket with paths staged explicitly.
- **Ops tickets (`O*`)** are yours: accounts, payments, consoles, testers. Claude can prepare text and checklists but can't sign up, pay or accept agreements.
- **A UI code ticket waits for its design ticket.** A code ticket that changes what users see starts only after the design ticket it names under **Waits for** is approved.
- **Done means verified:** on the Android emulator at least, and on an iPhone (via TestFlight) for anything touch-, keyboard- or notification-related.

## Design (`/ai-mobile-product-design-director`, redesign mode)

### D1 Mobile design brief (Gate 0)
- **Inputs:**
  - the spec;
  - D200 (phone layout);
  - `docs/mocks/momentum.html`;
  - `docs/design/momentum-tokens.css`;
  - screenshots of the current app at 390×844 and 412×915, taken from `npm run dev` in a phone-sized window.
- **Write:** the design ideas document. It covers:
  - audience and primary jobs on a phone: capture fast, see today, tick things off, check a habit, get reminded;
  - entry points: launcher, notification tap, later deep links;
  - one-hand use;
  - offline;
  - what must stay from desktop: the tokens, the accent presets, the visual rules in CLAUDE.md;
  - what changes: no keyboard shortcuts, no hover;
  - success criteria.
- **Questions to put to you, not guess:**
  - tablets as phone-only (as the spec says) or not;
  - whether the tour runs on mobile;
  - whether haptics are wanted.
- **Done:** brief approved.

### D2 Inventory, clutter audit and state model (Gate 1)
- **Observed inventory** of every phone screen: Today, Calendar (Day/Week/Month at phone width), Habits, the More menu and its destinations (Inbox, Upcoming, Focus, Progress, lists, Search, Settings), the task sheet, quick add with its tokens and chips, Settings pages, and Account sign-in and signed-in.
- **Clutter audit** per screen:
  - desktop leftovers: keycaps, hover-only tips, the Scale setting, window controls, Voice, the tray and launch-at-login rows;
  - competing actions, dense rows.
- **State matrix** for each screen, covering:
  - first launch;
  - signed out and signed in;
  - syncing, offline, sync error and update required;
  - empty, loading and long content;
  - keyboard open;
  - notification permission not asked, granted and denied (including revoked in system settings);
  - exact-alarm permission denied (Android);
  - deep entry from a notification to a task that was deleted meanwhile.
- **Done:** inventory, audit and state matrix approved, with a reduction plan (Keep / Combine / Move / Disclose / Remove-pending-confirmation).

### D3 Platform research and reference ledger (Gate 2)
- **References, studied by task and state** (rationale only, never copied):
  - task apps on both platforms: Apple Reminders, Things, Todoist, TickTick, Microsoft To Do, Google Tasks;
  - habit apps.
- **Decide:**
  - how much each platform differs, the "platform costume" question: one shared UI with platform overrides only where users expect them (back behaviour, date and time pickers, the share sheet, the notification permission prompt, haptics);
  - bottom bar versus anything else (D200 already has `PhoneBar`; confirm or challenge it);
  - full-screen task sheet versus a bottom sheet;
  - when to ask for notification permission.
- **Done:** a reference ledger, plus 2–3 directions with trade-offs; one approved.

### D4 Gestures (Gate 4)
- The spec proposes swipes on task rows, press-and-hold drag, and back gesture handling. Treat each as a hypothesis and fill in the skill's gesture checklist:
  - discoverability;
  - threshold;
  - conflict with scrolling, and with the iOS edge-swipe and Android back gesture;
  - accidental activation;
  - feedback and haptics;
  - undo (the app already has undo, `src/lib/undo.ts`);
  - a reduced-motion fallback;
  - **a visible control that does the same thing.**
- Reject any gesture that has no visible alternative or that only saves a tap nobody needs.
- **Done:** approved gesture spec; rejected gestures recorded as rejected alternatives.

### D5 Wireframes (Gate 4)
Low fidelity, at phone widths and with real, messy data: long titles, many tags, 30 tasks today, empty days. Screens:
- **Safe areas:** notch, Dynamic Island, Android gesture bar, landscape.
- **Quick add with the keyboard open:** token chips, the submit path, staying ready for the next task.
- **Task sheet:** properties, subtasks, notes with images, closing.
- **Settings on a phone:** which rows hide, which move.
- **Sign-in:** the one-time-code field (Mail and keyboard autofill), consent, the handle step (the `@` prefix from v1.1.1).
- **The moment and screen that ask for notification permission,** and what Settings shows after a denial (with a route to system settings).
- **Delete account:** confirmation, the 7-day grace period, the cancel banner on sign-in.
- **Update required:** links to the store.
- **First run:** no account, straight into Today; how the tour or Getting started adapts or is skipped.
- **Done:** wireframes approved per flow.

### D6 System deltas for mobile (Gate 3)
- **Reuse the existing tokens** (`src/styles/tokens.css`); only list the deltas:
  - safe-area variables;
  - `--touch` everywhere;
  - type behaviour with the system text size: iOS Dynamic Type doesn't reach a WKWebView by itself, while Android's font scale does reach the WebView. Decide whether the Scale setting maps to the system size or stays hidden.
- **Motion** on mobile follows the existing durations and easings, plus reduced motion.
- **Accessibility:** VoiceOver and TalkBack labels for icon-only controls, focus order in sheets, announcing changes in sync status.
- **Done:** deltas approved; no new colours or fonts unless approved.

### D7 Final screens and handoff (Gate 5)
- Annotated final screens for each wireframed flow.
- The state matrix filled in with feedback, announcements, retry and undo for each state.
- A component inventory that maps every piece to existing components (`src/components/ui/*`, `.btn`, `.input`, `.seg`, `.layer`…), so code tickets reuse rather than invent.
- **Done:** handoff approved. It is the input to C8, C9 and the UI parts of C7 and C11.

### D8 Store assets
- **App icon:** iOS needs a 1024px icon with no alpha; Android needs an adaptive icon (foreground plus background) and a monochrome icon. The mobile sets were removed in D42, so regenerate them from `assets/app-icon.svg` with `npx tauri icon`, and check each layer.
- **Screenshots:** 6.9" and 6.5" iPhone, plus Android phone. Use real-looking demo data (the seed), not mock-ups.
- **Listing text:** subtitle, short and full description, keywords; plain claims only.
- **Done:** assets approved and uploaded in O2.

### C7 Reminders and focus alerts as OS notifications
- **Pure function in `src/domain/reminders.ts`:**
  - name: `upcomingFires(now, days = 7, limit = 60)`;
  - built from the existing `remindersBetween`, `startsBetween` and `digestBetween`, plus `dedupeFires`;
  - returns the earliest `limit` fires;
  - **check:** a test beside it for the limit, ordering, de-duplication and a recurring series.
- **Mobile adapter** next to `reminderScheduler.ts`:
  1. cancel all pending notifications;
  2. schedule `upcomingFires` with `tauri-plugin-notification` `Schedule.at`;
  3. each notification's id is derived from the reminder `key`, and `extra` carries the `ReminderTarget`.
- **Reschedule on:** start, resume, `onWrite` (debounced like sync), and after a pull applies changes.
- **Tapping a notification** opens the target, through the existing `openTask` / `openEvent` / `go`. A target that was deleted gets the toast from D2's state matrix.
- **Focus:** starting a phase schedules one notification at its end; pause, skip and stop cancel it.
- **Permission:** ask on first use, per D5. Android needs `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`. For exact timing, use `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` or `USE_EXACT_ALARM`; decide which from Play policy and record it in PLAN.
- **Spike inside the ticket:** do Done and Snooze actions (`registerActionTypes`) reach a suspended app on both platforms? If not, ship tap-to-open only and record it.
- **Ladder notes:**
  - no server push;
  - no background tasks;
  - the desktop's 30 s loop is untouched (gated by `isMobile`);
  - `reminder_log` stays the "already fired" record.
- **Waits for:** D5 (permission moment and the denied state).
- **Done:** with the app closed, a reminder and a focus end arrive on time on both platforms, and tapping opens the right place.

### C11 Delete account (store blocker) and the 30-day content purge
- **Server** (`supabase/migrations/`):
  - a `deletion_requested_at` on `profiles`;
  - an RPC to request deletion and one to cancel it;
  - a scheduled job (`pg_cron`) that hard-deletes the owner's `sync_rows`, `devices` and `profiles` rows and the auth user after 7 days;
  - in the same job, clear the content of tombstoned rows older than 30 days, keeping `{id, deleted_at}`, as the Privacy policy promises.
- **Signing out every device** needs the service role, so it runs in that job or in one Edge Function. Never in the app.
- **Server tests** in `supabase/tests/sync.test.ts`:
  - request, then cancel within the grace period;
  - the purge keeps tombstones;
  - another account can't delete yours.
- **App:**
  - Settings → Account → Delete account, per D5;
  - signing in during the grace period shows the cancel banner;
  - signed-out devices get the existing "you were signed out" path.
- **Web:** `site/delete-account.html`, with steps and the support email.
- **Ladder notes:**
  - `pg_cron` and the existing RLS functions; no new backend service;
  - one job does both deletions.
- **Waits for:** D5 (the UI part only; the server part can start earlier).
- **Done:** an account deleted on the phone disappears after the grace period, and the desktop is told it was signed out.
