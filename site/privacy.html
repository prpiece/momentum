Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

The short version. Without an account, everything stays on your device and we never see it.

If you sign in, we store your email and the things you create in Momentum so they can sync between your devices.

We don't sell your data, show ads or run analytics.

Who we are

Momentum is a planner app run by an independent developer in Australia ("we", "us"). You can reach us at SUPPORT_EMAIL. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles, and give everyone the rights described below wherever they live, including people covered by the EU and UK GDPR.

Using Momentum without an account

Your tasks, lists, habits, calendar events, focus sessions and settings are stored only on your device. We don't receive any of it.

The app connects to the internet for two things only:

  • to check GitHub for a new version, which you can turn off under Settings → About;
  • to download the voice model, if you turn on voice input. Voice is processed on your device, and your recordings are never sent anywhere.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Account details: your email address, the @handle and display name you choose, and your avatar choice.
  • Your Momentum content: folders, lists, sections, tags, tasks and their notes, subtasks, checklists and reminders, task history, calendar events and countdowns, habits and check-ins, focus sessions, rest days, and settings that follow you between devices (such as theme and week start). Settings tied to one computer, like window layout and keyboard shortcuts, stay on that device. Note images (attachments) are not synced.
  • Devices: a random ID for each device you sign in on, its platform (Windows, macOS or Linux) and when it last synced, so you can see your signed-in devices.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider records IP addresses and request details in its server logs, for security and troubleshooting.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in: we email you a one-time code.
  • To sync your content between your devices.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems.

We don't use your content for advertising, profiling or training AI models. We don't send marketing emails. The only emails we send are sign-in codes, and messages about your account or important changes to these terms.

Under the GDPR, our legal basis is performing our contract with you (providing the service you signed up for), plus our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

Who processes it for us

We share your information only with these service providers, and only so they can run the service for us:

  • Supabase: hosts the database and handles sign-in.
  • Google (Gmail): delivers sign-in code emails.
  • GitHub: hosts these pages and the app's update downloads.

Their servers may be outside Australia, including in the United States, and they protect your data under their own security and privacy terms. We will also disclose information if the law requires it.

We never sell or rent your information.

How it's protected

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The database only lets each account read and change its own records.

Your content is not end-to-end encrypted. This means it's stored on our hosting provider's servers in a form that could, technically, be read by us or by that provider. We access it only when you ask us to help with a problem, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • While your account is open, we keep your account details and synced content.
  • When you delete something, it's removed from your other devices. Its content is cleared from our servers within 30 days. We keep a small marker with the item's ID, so a device that was offline still learns it was deleted.
  • When you delete your account, your account details and all synced content are deleted within 30 days. Your provider's encrypted backups roll over within a further 30 days or so.
  • Server logs are kept for a limited time, as set by our hosting provider.

Signing out doesn't delete anything on our servers. You can choose to keep a copy on your device, or remove it from your device.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information;
  • correct it;
  • delete it, or close your account;
  • tell you how we handle it.

You can also export your data yourself at any time under Settings → Data → Export backup.

To make a request, email SUPPORT_EMAIL from the address you sign in with. We reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), or to your local data protection authority if you're in the EU or UK.

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll also tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
