Delete your account

In the app

  • Phone: More → Account → Delete account.
  • Computer: Settings → Account → Delete account.

Every device signed in to your account is signed out. Seven days later, your account and everything synced to it are deleted for good. If you change your mind, sign in again before then and choose Keep my account.

Without the app

Email phanikumariparitala@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What's deleted

  • Your email address, handle and display name.
  • Everything synced to your account: tasks, lists, habits, calendar events, focus sessions and settings.
  • The list of your signed-in devices.

Nothing is kept on our servers after that. Our provider's encrypted backups roll over within about 30 more days. The copy of your data on your own devices stays there until you remove it or uninstall Momentum.
